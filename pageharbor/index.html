Android · Open source · Available on Google Play

Scan documents without giving them away.

PageHarbor is a privacy-first Android document scanner from SynapseWorks. Scan, recognize text, create PDFs, and choose where your files go.

View source on GitHub

PageHarbor is available on Google Play. Read the privacy policy.

Local-first by design

PageHarbor does not operate a document backend, cloud storage service, account system, advertising service, or its own analytics or tracking service.

What PageHarbor can do

A focused document workflow built around Android’s system interfaces and local processing.

Scan and review

  • Scan one or more pages through the Android scanner workflow.
  • Review and reorder pages when supported by the scanner result.
  • Import pages from the gallery where the current app flow supports it.

Export your way

  • Save normal PDFs through the Android Storage Access Framework.
  • Export individual pages as JPEG files.
  • Share PDFs through the Android Sharesheet.

Recognize and search

  • Run OCR locally and review recognized text in the active session.
  • Copy recognized text only after choosing Copy Text.
  • Create searchable PDFs locally with an invisible OCR text layer.

Privacy principles

No account or ads

PageHarbor does not require an account or login, and it has no advertising.

No PageHarbor tracking

There is no PageHarbor analytics, tracking service, proprietary cloud storage, or document backend.

Local processing

OCR and searchable-PDF generation happen on the device. PageHarbor does not maintain a document library.

You choose the destination

Exports and shares happen after your action through Android’s file picker or Sharesheet.

Google ML Kit is a separate SDK. Its documented technical diagnostics may be encrypted and sent to Google; this is not PageHarbor analytics and does not include document content.

How files are handled

While you work

Scan pages, OCR text, and prepared searchable PDFs are handled locally. Temporary app-private files may be created for processing, saving, sharing, retry, or cleanup.

When you export or share

You select the destination or receiving app. Android and any external storage provider, viewer, or share target handle the resulting file under their own policies.
