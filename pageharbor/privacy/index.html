Privacy Policy for PageHarbor

Last updated: 26 August 2026

Scope

This policy applies to the PageHarbor Android application published by SynapseWorks. It describes the current practices documented for the app and later versions that retain the same practices.

Use of synapseworks.org is covered separately by the site-wide privacy policy and cookie policy.

Summary

PageHarbor has no account, ads, PageHarbor backend, PageHarbor analytics, or PageHarbor tracking service.

Document images, OCR text, and searchable-PDF text layers are processed on the device. Exports and shares happen only after you choose a destination or receiving app.

Google ML Kit may transmit encrypted technical diagnostics according to Google’s SDK disclosure. Those diagnostics are separate from PageHarbor’s own code and do not include document content.

Information PageHarbor does not collect

PageHarbor does not create or require:

  • an account or profile;
  • access to your contacts, location, microphone, phone, or advertising ID;
  • PageHarbor analytics, tracking, advertising, or a server-side document upload;
  • a persistent PageHarbor document library, cloud copy, or sync service.

This does not mean that packaged platform SDKs can never transmit technical information. The ML Kit section below explains the documented diagnostics behavior.

Optional iOS interest on this website

The PageHarbor website offers an optional way to register interest in a possible iOS version. This website feature is separate from the Android app and does not mean that an iOS release is committed or scheduled.

  • Choosing Count me in stores only an anonymous interest row and its timestamp. It does not store document data, a device fingerprint, user agent, or raw IP address in the database.
  • After registering interest, you may optionally provide an email address. It is normalized and stored only to notify you if a possible PageHarbor iOS beta becomes available; it is not used for unrelated marketing.
  • Submitting either option uses Cloudflare Turnstile to protect the form from automated abuse. Turnstile processes the verification under Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy. SynapseWorks does not store the verification token, IP address, user agent, or fingerprint in this database.
  • Anonymous interest rows are retained only as an aggregate product-interest signal. You may request deletion of an optional email address by contacting privacy@synapseworks.org.

Document scanning and local processing

PageHarbor uses the Android/Google ML Kit document scanner workflow. The scanner provides the capture interaction, including its supported review tools. PageHarbor processes returned scan pages on the device.

  • OCR runs locally on the device, and recognized text is held in the active session unless you explicitly copy it.
  • Searchable PDFs are generated locally with an invisible OCR text layer.
  • PageHarbor can save normal PDFs and individual JPEG pages through Android’s system file picker.
  • PageHarbor does not directly request camera permission or broad storage/media access; the current manifest also declares no INTERNET permission.

Google ML Kit and Google Play services

PageHarbor uses Google ML Kit Document Scanner and bundled ML Kit Text Recognition. Google documents that document and OCR content processing occurs on-device and that ML Kit does not send document images, video, text input, or text-recognition output to Google servers.

ML Kit may send encrypted technical diagnostics to Google. The documented categories include device and app information, feature or configuration information, performance and error data, and per-installation identifiers used for diagnostics, reliability, compatibility, and improvement. This is SDK data collection outside PageHarbor’s own code; it is not advertising and does not include document content.

Google Play services may obtain scanner resources or updates. PageHarbor therefore does not promise that every Google-provided component can be acquired or updated without network access, even though PageHarbor itself declares no INTERNET permission and does not send document content to a server.

See Google’s ML Kit data-disclosure guidance and ML Kit terms and privacy information. Google’s services are governed by Google’s own terms and privacy practices.

Temporary files, retention, and deletion

Pages and OCR results are active-session data. A temporary searchable PDF or share copy may be held in PageHarbor’s private app cache only while it is needed for a requested save, share, retry, or cleanup operation.

  • Prepared searchable PDFs are removed after success, failure, cancellation, or discard.
  • Completed share copies are cache data and are cleaned when stale.
  • PageHarbor does not maintain an internal document library or PageHarbor-hosted user-data record.
  • Android backup and device transfer for PageHarbor’s private data are disabled.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its app-private data through Android.

Export, storage, and external providers

PageHarbor uses Android’s Storage Access Framework. You choose where a file is saved through the system file picker. A provider such as local storage or an installed cloud-storage app handles the selected file under its own terms and privacy policy.

PageHarbor does not receive the provider’s account credentials and cannot control a provider’s retention, security, or privacy practices. Files saved through a selected provider remain under that provider and your control; PageHarbor does not delete them.

Sharing and external viewers

When you choose Share, PageHarbor uses Android’s Sharesheet. You choose the receiving application, and that application receives the file only after your action. External viewers and share targets have their own policies and handling practices.

Clipboard

Recognized text is placed on the Android clipboard only when you choose Copy Text. Android and other apps may affect clipboard access and retention, so avoid copying sensitive text unnecessarily.

Permissions

PageHarbor’s current manifest declares no INTERNET, broad-storage, account, location, contacts, microphone, phone, or advertising permissions. PageHarbor does not request camera permission directly; the Google-provided scanner flow owns its camera interaction.

Files shared through PageHarbor use a non-exported Android FileProvider with temporary read access limited to the cache subdirectory used for share copies. Permission behavior can change with a future app feature or dependency, and this policy will be updated if it does.

Children

PageHarbor is a general-purpose document utility and is not specifically directed at children. PageHarbor does not collect age information.

Security

PageHarbor uses local-first processing, app-private temporary files, scoped Android file access, and cleanup of prepared outputs. No method of storage or transfer is absolutely secure, so use Android and destination-provider controls appropriate to your documents.

For security reports, contact security@synapseworks.org or visit the SynapseWorks security page.

Changes to this policy

If PageHarbor changes its permissions, data handling, dependencies, storage, or sharing behavior, SynapseWorks will update this policy and the applicable Google Play Data safety declaration before distributing the changed build. The date at the top of this page will change when the policy is updated.
