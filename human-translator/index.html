A phrasebook with a sense of humour

Choose Corporate ↔ Human, Gen Z ↔ Millennial, or Polite ↔ Direct. There are 216 Corporate, 80 Gen Z and 119 Polite selectable riffs, with themes for acronyms, email, internet slang and roasts. Pick a phrase, try the reverse direction or let “Surprise me” choose one. There is no free-text input.

The punchlines are original satire. References document expressions or language families; they do not verify the joke or prove current virality. Slang overlaps between generations and changes with context. Roasts are labelled, and these are not reliable readings of anyone's intentions or advice for sensitive conversations.

Shared links contain only a permanent curated phrase ID and direction in the URL fragment. Earlier shared examples retain their original wording. Social previews show the same generic card; the result appears when the link opens.

Your choices and optional usage counts

Human Translator has no translation server, account, saved phrase history or database. Copy and share send only the curated result you choose to your clipboard or share app.

Optional website analytics use the site's existing Cloudflare Zaraz integration only when its Analytics purpose is explicitly granted. Human Translator events contain fixed action labels, mode and direction, never message text or individual phrase IDs. Events stay off on previews, with Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, or when consent cannot be verified. Normal website delivery still uses Cloudflare.

A share-button action is not a recipient visit. Link-tagged visits are counted separately and do not prove a unique person, delivery or an app installation. See the website privacy policy and cookie policy.
