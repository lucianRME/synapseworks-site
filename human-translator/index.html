A small phrasebook with a sense of humour

Choose Corporate ↔ Human, Gen Z ↔ Millennial, or Polite ↔ Direct. Each mode has 12 original pairs that work in both directions. Matching ignores letter case, extra spaces, curly quotes and final sentence punctuation. Other wording gets an honest “no curated match”.

These are playful English examples, not universal definitions of a generation or a reliable reading of someone's intentions. Slang overlaps between ages and changes with context. This is not advice for sensitive conversations.

Shared links contain only a curated phrase ID and direction in the URL fragment. They do not include what you typed. Social previews show the same generic card; the result appears when the link opens.

Your text and optional usage counts

Human Translator has no translation server, account, history or database. Typed text stays in this page's memory until you leave. Copy and share send only the curated result you choose to your clipboard or share app.

Optional website analytics use the site's existing Cloudflare Zaraz integration only when its Analytics purpose is explicitly granted. Human Translator events contain fixed action labels, mode and direction, never your text. Events stay off on previews, with Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, or when consent cannot be verified. Normal website delivery still uses Cloudflare.

A share-button action is not a recipient visit. Link-tagged visits are counted separately and do not prove a unique person, delivery or an app installation. See the website privacy policy and cookie policy.
