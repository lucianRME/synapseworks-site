RME: PDF & Document Scanner

Private, local-first Android scanning with on-device OCR, searchable PDFs, Share-Safe redaction and a document library that stays on your device.

Help & FAQ  →
Get RME on Google Play View source on GitHub

Latest stable release · Android phones and tablets · Download the APK or view the latest release · Read the privacy policy.

Local-first by design

Documents saved to RME stay in an on-device library you can search, organize, back up and move. RME has no document backend, account system, ads, or RME analytics and tracking.

Formerly PageHarbor.

From capture to a useful local library

Scan paper, import files, recognize text, organize documents and prepare the PDF you need without sending documents to an RME backend.

Scan and organize

Capture multiple pages, import PDFs or images through Android Share or the system file picker, and save documents in a local library with folders, subfolders, rename, move and smart local naming.

OCR and search

Run multilingual on-device OCR, review and correct text, explicitly re-run OCR, recognize documents in batches, search recognized text across the library and create searchable PDFs.

PDF tools

Merge documents, split or extract pages, extract recognized text, create searchable PDFs and prepare smaller files with Fit Upload Limit.

Move and protect

Import PDFs or images exported by other scanner apps, make a portable or password-encrypted backup, restore on a new phone and optionally protect app access with Android device authentication.

Share-Safe: prepare a copy before it leaves your device

Create a separate Share-Safe copy with irreversible blackout redaction and a purpose or date watermark. Drag redactions directly, use fit-relative pinch zoom and pan for precise placement, then inspect the final preview before Save or Share.

The saved library document remains separate from the prepared copy. External storage providers and receiving apps handle files you choose to give them under their own terms.

Fit common PDF upload limits

Prepare a PDF for limits such as 200 KB, 500 KB, 1 MB, 2 MB or a custom target. RME uses readability-aware fitting and shows the final result before you save or share it.

Very small targets may not be achievable for every document; page count, image detail and readable quality set practical limits.

Two-sided ID and card copy

Place the front and back of a card on A4 or Letter paper with Auto, Vertical or Horizontal layout options. Redact details where needed and review the final copy before saving or sharing.

Bring user-exported files from another scanner

Import standard PDFs and supported images that you exported or shared from CamScanner, Adobe Scan or Acrobat, Genius Scan, Microsoft Lens and other scanner apps.

Migration uses files you choose through Android’s standard file and sharing interfaces. RME does not access another app’s account, private database or proprietary archive.

On-device OCR, with a clear delivery boundary

Latin OCR is bundled. Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Devanagari use optional models that may be obtained through Google Play services and are then used for on-device recognition.

Google Play services may also need a connection to obtain or update scanner components. See the RME privacy policy for the distinction between local document processing and Google SDK diagnostics.

Get RME for Android

RME is available on Google Play. The source and latest stable release APK are also published on GitHub.

Get RME on Google Play View the latest GitHub release Install with Obtainium

Obtainium follows RME's stable GitHub Releases and is an alternative to Google Play. Its button uses Obtainium's third-party hosted redirect, which includes analytics and advertising scripts. To avoid that redirect, add https://github.com/lucianRME/rme-pdf-scanner manually in Obtainium and keep prereleases disabled.

The GitHub APK and Google Play build use different signing certificates, so Android cannot install one channel over the other as an update. Before changing channels, create a portable RME backup and confirm that you can access it; removing the installed app also removes its app-private document library.

Privacy by design

On-device library

Documents you save to RME and any OCR text you choose to index are stored locally in app-private data.

No RME cloud backend

RME has no backend for receiving documents and no proprietary cloud sync service.

No account or ads

The Android app requires no RME account and includes no advertising.

No RME analytics

RME does not operate its own analytics or tracking service.

No app network permissions

The RME app requests neither INTERNET nor ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE.

Additional OCR models may be downloaded by Google Play services. Document recognition runs on-device. Google ML Kit’s documented technical diagnostics may be sent to Google; this is separate from RME-operated analytics and does not include document content. Read the full privacy disclosure.
