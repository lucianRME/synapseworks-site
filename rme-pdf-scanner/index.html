RME: PDF & Document Scanner

A privacy-first PDF and document scanner for Android. Scan paper, import files, organize documents locally, and make PDFs searchable with offline OCR.

Get RME on Google Play View source on GitHub

Android phones and tablets · v1.4.0 features. Read the privacy policy.

Local-first by design

Documents saved to RME stay in an on-device library. RME does not operate a document backend, cloud sync, account system, advertising service, or its own analytics or tracking service.

Formerly PageHarbor.

Private document scanning on Android

Capture paper pages through Google ML Kit Document Scanner, then review them in RME on an Android phone or tablet. Scan processing happens on the device; using RME does not require a proprietary cloud account.

Google Play services may need a connection to obtain or update scanner components. See the RME privacy policy for the distinction between local document processing and Google SDK diagnostics.

Import, merge and split PDFs

Bring existing JPEG, PNG, WebP and PDF files into the same review flow as new scans. Choose multiple files with Android’s system picker, or send supported files to RME through Android sharing.

Combine saved documents in your chosen order. Extract selected pages to a new document, or move pages out of one saved document when you need to split it. Imported source files remain with their original provider.

Offline OCR and searchable PDFs

Bundled Latin-script text recognition runs on the device. Review or copy recognized text after you start OCR, and generate a PDF with a selectable, searchable text layer.

OCR accuracy depends on page quality and supported scripts. The text layer reflects the recognition result, so check important details against the original page.

Organize documents locally

Choose Save to RME to keep a document in the private on-device library. Unsaved sessions are not silently retained.

Rename saved documents, move them into folders, search titles and text from OCR you have run, and sort them. The library presents documents in a list or grid layout according to the available space. Saved documents can be reopened for editing and export.

Edit pages before export

Preview pages in the document review flow. Reorder, rotate, remove or add pages, then choose Original, Enhance, Grayscale, Black and white, or High Contrast filters where useful.

Export and share

Save a standard or searchable PDF, or export individual pages as JPEG files. Android’s system file picker lets you choose the save destination; the Sharesheet lets you choose a receiving app for PDF sharing.

External storage providers and share targets handle the files you give them under their own terms.

Privacy by design

On-device library

Documents you save to RME and any OCR text you choose to index are stored locally in app-private data.

No RME cloud backend

RME has no backend for receiving documents and no proprietary cloud sync service.

No account or ads

The Android app requires no RME account and includes no advertising.

No RME analytics

RME does not operate its own analytics or tracking service.

Google ML Kit is a separate SDK. Its documented technical diagnostics may be sent to Google; this is separate from RME-operated analytics and does not include document content. Read the full privacy disclosure.
