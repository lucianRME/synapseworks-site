RME: PDF & Document Scanner

Private, local-first Android document scanning with on-device OCR, searchable PDFs, full-library search, migration, backup and document ownership.

Get RME on Google Play View source on GitHub

Version 1.6.0 · Android phones and tablets · Read the privacy policy.

Local-first by design

Documents saved to RME stay in an on-device library you can search, organize, back up and move. RME has no document backend, account system, ads, or RME analytics and tracking.

Formerly PageHarbor.

Private document scanning on Android

Capture paper pages through Google ML Kit Document Scanner, then review them in RME on an Android phone or tablet. Scan processing happens on the device; using RME does not require a proprietary cloud account.

Google Play services may need a connection to obtain or update scanner components. See the RME privacy policy for the distinction between local document processing and Google SDK diagnostics.

Import, migrate, merge and split PDFs

Bring existing JPEG, PNG, WebP and PDF files into the same review flow as new scans. Choose multiple files with Android’s system picker, or send supported files to RME through Android sharing.

Move exported PDFs and images from CamScanner, Adobe Scan or Acrobat, Genius Scan, Microsoft Lens and other scanners through RME’s guided migration workflow. This uses ordinary Android file and sharing interfaces, not access to another app’s account or private database.

Combine saved documents in your chosen order. Extract selected pages to a new document, or move pages out of one saved document when you need to split it. Imported source files remain with their original provider.

Multilingual on-device OCR and searchable PDFs

Latin OCR is built in. Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Devanagari OCR are available through optional Google Play services model delivery. Document recognition runs on-device.

Review and correct recognized text, re-run OCR for a page or document, or recognize selected documents in a batch. RME can create a PDF with a selectable, searchable text layer from the effective OCR text.

OCR accuracy depends on page quality and supported scripts. The text layer reflects the recognition result, so check important details against the original page.

Search and organize your document library

Choose Save to RME to keep a document in the private on-device library. Unsaved sessions are not silently retained.

Find documents by title or recognized text across the full library. Results include matching snippets and can open the matching page directly. Rename saved documents, use folders and subfolders, apply local smart naming suggestions, and sort the library. Saved documents can be reopened for editing and export.

Back up, restore and move your library

Create a complete portable RME backup and restore it on this or another Android phone. The backup preserves documents, folders, source assets, OCR state and corrections.

Choose an unencrypted backup or protect the portable backup with a password. Android automatic cloud backup and device transfer remain disabled; backup and restore happen only when you choose them through Android’s system file picker.

Optional app lock uses Android device authentication to protect access through the RME interface. It does not encrypt the local library at rest.

Edit pages before export

Preview pages in the document review flow. Reorder, rotate, remove or add pages, then choose Original, Enhance, Grayscale, Black and white, or High Contrast filters where useful.

Export and share

Save a standard or searchable PDF, or export individual pages as JPEG files. Android’s system file picker lets you choose the save destination; the Sharesheet lets you choose a receiving app for PDF sharing.

External storage providers and share targets handle the files you give them under their own terms.

Privacy by design

On-device library

Documents you save to RME and any OCR text you choose to index are stored locally in app-private data.

No RME cloud backend

RME has no backend for receiving documents and no proprietary cloud sync service.

No account or ads

The Android app requires no RME account and includes no advertising.

No RME analytics

RME does not operate its own analytics or tracking service.

No app network permissions

The RME app requests neither INTERNET nor ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE.

Additional OCR models may be downloaded by Google Play services. Document recognition runs on-device. Google ML Kit’s documented technical diagnostics may be sent to Google; this is separate from RME-operated analytics and does not include document content. Read the full privacy disclosure.
