Privacy Policy for RME: Private PDF Scanner

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Scope

This policy applies to the current release of RME: Private PDF Scanner, the Android application published by SynapseWorks, and to later releases that retain the same practices.

Use of synapseworks.org is covered separately by the site-wide privacy policy and cookie policy.

Summary

RME: Private PDF Scanner has no account, ads, proprietary document backend, analytics, or tracking service.

Document images, OCR text, search indexes, and searchable-PDF text layers are processed on the device. Documents explicitly saved to RME are kept in its app-private on-device library; RME has no backend that receives or stores them and provides no proprietary cloud sync. Exports, portable backups, and shares happen after you choose a destination or receiving app.

Google ML Kit may transmit encrypted technical diagnostics according to Google’s SDK disclosure. Those diagnostics are separate from RME: Private PDF Scanner’s own code and do not include document content.

Data and services RME does not use

RME: Private PDF Scanner does not require or operate:

  • an account or profile;
  • access to your contacts, location, microphone, phone, or advertising ID;
  • its own analytics, tracking, or advertising service;
  • an RME-operated document upload, hosted copy, or sync service.

This does not mean that packaged platform SDKs can never transmit technical information. The ML Kit section below explains the documented diagnostics behavior.

Optional iOS interest on this website

The RME: Private PDF Scanner website offers an optional way to register interest in a possible iOS version. This website feature is separate from the Android app and does not mean that an iOS release is committed or scheduled.

  • Choosing Count me in stores only an anonymous interest row and its timestamp. It does not store document data, a device fingerprint, user agent, or raw IP address in the database.
  • After registering interest, you may optionally provide an email address. It is normalized and stored only to notify you if a possible RME: Private PDF Scanner iOS beta becomes available; it is not used for unrelated marketing.
  • Submitting either option uses Cloudflare Turnstile to protect the form from automated abuse. Turnstile processes the verification under Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy. SynapseWorks does not store the verification token, IP address, user agent, or fingerprint in this database.
  • Anonymous interest rows are retained only as an aggregate product-interest signal. You may request deletion of an optional email address by contacting privacy@synapseworks.org.

Document scanning and local processing

RME: Private PDF Scanner uses the Android/Google ML Kit document scanner workflow. The scanner provides the capture interaction. RME processes returned pages on the device and also accepts user-selected images and PDFs, including multiple files, or supported files shared from another Android app.

  • OCR runs locally on the device. Recognized text and user corrections can be stored and indexed for full-library search; text is copied to another app only after you choose Copy Text.
  • Searchable PDFs are generated locally with an invisible OCR text layer.
  • Share-Safe copies, blackout redactions, watermarks, Fit Upload Limit outputs, and two-sided ID or card copies are prepared on the device and shown in a final preview before you choose Save or Share.
  • RME: Private PDF Scanner can save standard or searchable PDFs and individual JPEG pages through Android’s system file picker.
  • RME: Private PDF Scanner does not directly request camera permission or broad storage/media access; the current manifest requests neither INTERNET nor ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE.

Google ML Kit and Google Play services

RME: Private PDF Scanner uses Google ML Kit Document Scanner, bundled Latin text recognition, and optional Google Play services text-recognition models for Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Devanagari. Additional OCR models may be downloaded by Google Play services. Document recognition runs on-device. Google documents that ML Kit does not send document images, video, text input, or text-recognition output to Google servers.

ML Kit may send encrypted technical diagnostics to Google. The documented categories include device and app information, feature or configuration information, performance and error data, and per-installation identifiers used for diagnostics, reliability, compatibility, and improvement. This is SDK data collection outside RME: Private PDF Scanner’s own code; it is not advertising and does not include document content.

Google Play services may obtain scanner resources, optional OCR models, or updates. RME: Private PDF Scanner therefore does not promise that every Google-provided component can be acquired or updated without network access, even though the RME app itself requests neither INTERNET nor ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and does not send document content to a server.

See Google’s ML Kit data-disclosure guidance and ML Kit terms and privacy information. Google’s services are governed by Google’s own terms and privacy practices.

Temporary files, retention, and deletion

Unsaved pages and OCR results begin as active-session data; an unsaved session is not recovered after process death. PDF import can use temporary app-private source and rendered page files. A temporary searchable PDF or share copy may be held in app cache while it is needed for a requested operation.

  • When you explicitly choose Save to RME, the app stores page copies, source assets where required for portability, thumbnails, title, timestamps, page order, editing state, folder assignment and OCR indexing status in its private on-device library. Recognized text and saved corrections are also stored locally.
  • Saved library data remains until you delete the saved document or remove the app’s data. Deleting a saved document removes RME’s copy and indexed text; it does not delete the source file you imported. Deleting a folder leaves its documents in the library.
  • Prepared searchable PDFs are removed after success, failure, cancellation or discard. Completed share copies and orphaned temporary files are cleaned when stale.
  • RME’s Android configuration disables automatic platform backup and excludes app-private data from cloud backup and device transfer. This is separate from the explicit portable backup described below.

Portable backup, restore, and migration

When you choose to create a portable backup, RME prepares a complete logical copy of the local library and writes it to a destination you select through Android’s system file picker. The backup can include documents, folders, source assets, OCR state and saved corrections. An unencrypted backup is an inspectable archive; an optional encrypted backup is protected with a password you provide.

RME does not upload portable backups to an RME service. If you select a cloud-storage provider in Android’s picker, that provider receives the file under its own terms. The backup password is not sent to RME, stored by an RME account, or recoverable by SynapseWorks. Restore verifies a selected backup before replacing the live library; incomplete or rejected restores do not become the active library.

The guided scanner-migration workflow reads only the standard PDFs or supported images that you select or share. It does not sign in to another scanner, access another app’s private database, or connect directly to Microsoft, OneDrive, CamScanner, Adobe, or Genius Scan accounts.

Export, storage, and external providers

RME: Private PDF Scanner uses Android’s Storage Access Framework to select imports and export destinations. You choose the files to import and where exports are saved through the system file picker. A provider such as local storage or an installed cloud-storage app handles selected files under its own terms and privacy policy.

RME: Private PDF Scanner does not receive the provider’s account credentials and cannot control a provider’s retention, security, or privacy practices. Files saved through a selected provider remain under that provider and your control; RME: Private PDF Scanner does not delete them.

Sharing and external viewers

RME: Private PDF Scanner can receive supported images and PDFs shared from another Android app. When you choose to share an export, RME uses Android’s Sharesheet; you choose the receiving application. External viewers and share targets have their own policies and handling practices.

Clipboard

Recognized text is placed on the Android clipboard only when you choose Copy Text. Android and other apps may affect clipboard access and retention, so avoid copying sensitive text unnecessarily.

Permissions

RME: Private PDF Scanner’s current manifest requests neither INTERNET nor ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, and declares no broad-storage, account, location, contacts, microphone, phone, or advertising permissions. RME: Private PDF Scanner does not request camera permission directly; the Google-provided scanner flow owns its camera interaction.

Files shared through RME: Private PDF Scanner use a non-exported Android FileProvider with temporary read access limited to the URI the app grants. Its configured roots cover private cache areas used for imports and exports and private library pages needed to reopen saved documents. Permission behavior can change with a future app feature or dependency, and this policy will be updated if it does.

Children

RME: Private PDF Scanner is a general-purpose document utility and is not specifically directed at children. RME: Private PDF Scanner does not collect age information.

Security

RME: Private PDF Scanner uses local processing, app-private library and temporary files, scoped Android file access, and cleanup of prepared outputs. Optional app lock uses Android device authentication to protect access through the RME interface; it does not encrypt the library at rest. Optional portable-backup encryption protects the backup file with the password you choose. No method of storage or transfer is absolutely secure, so use Android and destination-provider controls appropriate to your documents.

For security reports, contact security@synapseworks.org or visit the SynapseWorks security page.

Changes to this policy

If RME: Private PDF Scanner changes its permissions, data handling, dependencies, storage, or sharing behavior, SynapseWorks will update this policy and the applicable Google Play Data safety declaration before distributing the changed build. The date at the top of this page will change when the policy is updated.
