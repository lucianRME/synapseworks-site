Privacy Policy for RME: PDF & Document Scanner

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Scope

This policy applies to RME: PDF & Document Scanner, the Android application published by SynapseWorks. It describes the current v1.4.0 practices and later versions that retain the same practices.

Use of synapseworks.org is covered separately by the site-wide privacy policy and cookie policy.

Summary

RME PDF Scanner has no account, ads, proprietary document backend, analytics, or tracking service.

Document images, OCR text, and searchable-PDF text layers are processed on the device. Documents explicitly saved to RME are kept in its app-private on-device library; RME has no backend that receives or stores them and provides no proprietary cloud sync. Exports and shares happen after you choose a destination or receiving app.

Google ML Kit may transmit encrypted technical diagnostics according to Google’s SDK disclosure. Those diagnostics are separate from RME PDF Scanner’s own code and do not include document content.

Data and services RME does not use

RME PDF Scanner does not require or operate:

  • an account or profile;
  • access to your contacts, location, microphone, phone, or advertising ID;
  • its own analytics, tracking, or advertising service;
  • an RME-operated document upload, hosted copy, or sync service.

This does not mean that packaged platform SDKs can never transmit technical information. The ML Kit section below explains the documented diagnostics behavior.

Optional iOS interest on this website

The RME PDF Scanner website offers an optional way to register interest in a possible iOS version. This website feature is separate from the Android app and does not mean that an iOS release is committed or scheduled.

  • Choosing Count me in stores only an anonymous interest row and its timestamp. It does not store document data, a device fingerprint, user agent, or raw IP address in the database.
  • After registering interest, you may optionally provide an email address. It is normalized and stored only to notify you if a possible RME PDF Scanner iOS beta becomes available; it is not used for unrelated marketing.
  • Submitting either option uses Cloudflare Turnstile to protect the form from automated abuse. Turnstile processes the verification under Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy. SynapseWorks does not store the verification token, IP address, user agent, or fingerprint in this database.
  • Anonymous interest rows are retained only as an aggregate product-interest signal. You may request deletion of an optional email address by contacting privacy@synapseworks.org.

Document scanning and local processing

RME PDF Scanner uses the Android/Google ML Kit document scanner workflow. The scanner provides the capture interaction. RME processes returned pages on the device and also accepts user-selected images and PDFs, including multiple files, or supported files shared from another Android app.

  • OCR runs locally on the device. Recognized text can be copied only after you choose Copy Text; text from OCR you run can also be stored and indexed when you save the document to RME.
  • Searchable PDFs are generated locally with an invisible OCR text layer.
  • RME PDF Scanner can save standard or searchable PDFs and individual JPEG pages through Android’s system file picker.
  • RME PDF Scanner does not directly request camera permission or broad storage/media access; the current manifest also declares no INTERNET permission.

Google ML Kit and Google Play services

RME PDF Scanner uses Google ML Kit Document Scanner and bundled ML Kit Text Recognition. Google documents that document and OCR content processing occurs on-device and that ML Kit does not send document images, video, text input, or text-recognition output to Google servers.

ML Kit may send encrypted technical diagnostics to Google. The documented categories include device and app information, feature or configuration information, performance and error data, and per-installation identifiers used for diagnostics, reliability, compatibility, and improvement. This is SDK data collection outside RME PDF Scanner’s own code; it is not advertising and does not include document content.

Google Play services may obtain scanner resources or updates. RME PDF Scanner therefore does not promise that every Google-provided component can be acquired or updated without network access, even though RME PDF Scanner itself declares no INTERNET permission and does not send document content to a server.

See Google’s ML Kit data-disclosure guidance and ML Kit terms and privacy information. Google’s services are governed by Google’s own terms and privacy practices.

Temporary files, retention, and deletion

Unsaved pages and OCR results begin as active-session data; an unsaved session is not recovered after process death. PDF import can use temporary app-private source and rendered page files. A temporary searchable PDF or share copy may be held in app cache while it is needed for a requested operation.

  • When you explicitly choose Save to RME, the app stores page copies, thumbnails, title, timestamps, page order, editing state, folder assignment and OCR indexing status in its private on-device library. Recognized text is also stored locally if you have run OCR for that document.
  • Saved library data remains until you delete the saved document or remove the app’s data. Deleting a saved document removes RME’s copy and indexed text; it does not delete the source file you imported. Deleting a folder leaves its documents in the library.
  • Prepared searchable PDFs are removed after success, failure, cancellation or discard. Completed share copies and orphaned temporary files are cleaned when stale.
  • RME’s current Android configuration disables backup and excludes app-private data from cloud backup and device transfer. This describes RME’s configuration, not files you independently export to another provider.

Export, storage, and external providers

RME PDF Scanner uses Android’s Storage Access Framework to select imports and export destinations. You choose the files to import and where exports are saved through the system file picker. A provider such as local storage or an installed cloud-storage app handles selected files under its own terms and privacy policy.

RME PDF Scanner does not receive the provider’s account credentials and cannot control a provider’s retention, security, or privacy practices. Files saved through a selected provider remain under that provider and your control; RME PDF Scanner does not delete them.

Sharing and external viewers

RME PDF Scanner can receive supported images and PDFs shared from another Android app. When you choose to share an export, RME uses Android’s Sharesheet; you choose the receiving application. External viewers and share targets have their own policies and handling practices.

Clipboard

Recognized text is placed on the Android clipboard only when you choose Copy Text. Android and other apps may affect clipboard access and retention, so avoid copying sensitive text unnecessarily.

Permissions

RME PDF Scanner’s current manifest declares no INTERNET, broad-storage, account, location, contacts, microphone, phone, or advertising permissions. RME PDF Scanner does not request camera permission directly; the Google-provided scanner flow owns its camera interaction.

Files shared through RME PDF Scanner use a non-exported Android FileProvider with temporary read access limited to the URI the app grants. Its configured roots cover private cache areas used for imports and exports and private library pages needed to reopen saved documents. Permission behavior can change with a future app feature or dependency, and this policy will be updated if it does.

Children

RME PDF Scanner is a general-purpose document utility and is not specifically directed at children. RME PDF Scanner does not collect age information.

Security

RME PDF Scanner uses local processing, app-private library and temporary files, scoped Android file access, and cleanup of prepared outputs. No method of storage or transfer is absolutely secure, so use Android and destination-provider controls appropriate to your documents.

For security reports, contact security@synapseworks.org or visit the SynapseWorks security page.

Changes to this policy

If RME PDF Scanner changes its permissions, data handling, dependencies, storage, or sharing behavior, SynapseWorks will update this policy and the applicable Google Play Data safety declaration before distributing the changed build. The date at the top of this page will change when the policy is updated.
