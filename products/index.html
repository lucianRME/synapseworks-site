Privacy-first browser tools

Small, practical browser tools for safer everyday workflows.

Submit Guard

A lightweight Chrome extension that helps prevent accidental form submissions and irreversible workflow actions.

  • Local-only, with no backend and no account.
  • Settings stored locally in chrome.storage.local.
  • No form contents are stored or transmitted.

Android tools

Local-first utilities for focused document workflows.

PageHarbor

An open-source Android document scanner with on-device OCR, searchable-PDF generation, and user-controlled export.

  • No account, ads, PageHarbor backend, or PageHarbor analytics.
  • Normal PDFs and JPEG pages save through Android’s system picker.
  • Preparing for internal testing; not yet publicly available on Google Play.
