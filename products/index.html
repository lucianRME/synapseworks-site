RME: PDF & Document Scanner

Turn paper and imported files into organized, searchable PDFs. Keep your document workflow in an on-device library.

  • Scan or import documents, recognize text, merge and split PDFs, and export PDF or JPEG files.
  • Process documents on your device, with no RME document backend. Source code is public on GitHub.
  • Google Play services may need a connection for scanner components or optional OCR models; recognition runs on-device.

Submit Guard

Add a confirmation step before sending important forms. Choose which sites to protect and reduce accidental submissions.

  • Confirm native form submissions, with an optional advanced mode for submit-like clicks in web apps.
  • Runs locally in Chrome, with local settings and no SynapseWorks account or backend.
  • The extension does not store or transmit form contents.

Jira · Decision visibility Early-stage

Decision Register for Jira

Make important Jira decisions easier to find and review, using issue history to reveal changes to plans and priorities.

  • Review changes to estimates, priorities, scope, assignees, due dates, and fix versions; export results as CSV.
  • Runs on Atlassian Forge with read-only access patterns and limited per-user preferences in Forge storage.
  • No external backend, external storage, or external network egress.

Jira · Delivery workflows Early-stage

Synapse for Jira

Bring structure to Jira requirements with BDD-style scenarios and aligned subtasks. Explore a more consistent approach to backlog refinement.

  • Refine Jira issue context into scenarios and subtasks to support delivery work.
  • Runs on Atlassian Forge, processing Jira issue context within Atlassian infrastructure.
  • No external backend or external storage; no stored Jira credentials, personal access tokens, or shared secrets.
