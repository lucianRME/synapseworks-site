Chrome Extensions

Local-first Chrome extensions for browser workflows

SynapseWorks browser tools are separate from the Jira app portfolio. They do not run on Atlassian unless a specific product page says so.

Submit Guard

Submit Guard is a Chrome extension that adds confirmation before form submissions on sites you enable.

  • Local-only processing.
  • No SynapseWorks backend and no SynapseWorks account.
  • Settings stored locally in chrome.storage.local.
  • No form contents stored or transmitted by the extension.
