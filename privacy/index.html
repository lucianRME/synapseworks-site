Scope

This Privacy Policy covers synapseworks.org (the website) and SynapseWorks’ general handling of personal data.

Each product has its own privacy information because its data handling depends on its platform and architecture. The links below take you to the relevant product disclosures.

Submit Guard (Chrome extension): Privacy policy

RME: Private PDF Scanner: Product-specific privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-21

SynapseWorks builds products across Chrome, Android, and Atlassian. The remaining sections describe the shared website practices and the Atlassian app data boundaries.

Atlassian app data boundaries

  • No Jira credentials, passwords, or personal access tokens are stored.
  • Access is scoped through Atlassian Forge appUserToken and permissions.
  • Data is processed only to deliver product functionality.

Decision Register for Jira (Forge-only)

Decision Register for Jira is Forge-only with no external backend, no external storage, and no external network egress.

  • Data read: Jira issue and decision context required to display and link decisions.
  • Data stored: Limited user preferences may be stored via Forge storage (storage:app).
  • External processing: None. No external backend, no external storage, and no external network egress.

Synapse Forge (Runs on Atlassian)

Synapse Forge is a Jira app that runs on Atlassian using a fully Forge-native runtime. Jira issue context is processed within Forge context and returned to Jira.

  • Data processed: Jira issue context required to generate structured outputs.
  • External processing: None. No external backend, no external storage, and no external network egress.
  • Auth model: Forge appUserToken and scoped permissions.
  • Credentials: No Jira credentials, personal access tokens, or shared secrets are stored.

Synapse data flow: Jira → Atlassian Forge runtime → Jira.

Analytics and consent (edge-managed)

Analytics for synapseworks.org is managed at the Cloudflare edge (for example, via Zaraz with Google Analytics 4). Analytics is disabled by default and only enabled after you opt in. We do not embed third-party analytics libraries directly in this site repository.

Human Translator matches curated phrases in your browser. Its optional usage events contain fixed action labels, translation mode and direction, and predefined outcome, source or destination values. They do not contain typed text, translation text, clipboard contents or visitor identifiers added by the tool. Events are sent through the existing Zaraz integration only when its Analytics consent purpose is explicitly granted; they stay off on previews and when Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control is enabled. Share actions and link-tagged visits are counted separately and do not establish message delivery or app installations.

Optional RME: Private PDF Scanner iOS interest

The RME: Private PDF Scanner website offers an optional interest signal for a possible iOS version. Registering interest stores an anonymous timestamped row; it does not store document data, a device fingerprint, user agent, or raw IP address in the database.

You may optionally provide an email address solely to be notified if a possible RME: Private PDF Scanner iOS beta becomes available. It is not used for unrelated marketing. We retain it only while that possible notification remains relevant, and will delete it earlier on a verified request sent to privacy@synapseworks.org.

Submitting the RME: Private PDF Scanner interest form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to protect it from automated abuse. Turnstile processes the verification under Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy; SynapseWorks does not store the verification token, IP address, user agent, or fingerprint in this database.

Retention

Operational logs are retained only as long as required for troubleshooting and security monitoring. Product outputs reside in your Jira site. Where analytics is enabled by consent, event retention is managed through standard GA4 controls.

Legal basis and consent

For visitors in the EU/EEA and UK, analytics processing is based on your consent. You can withdraw consent at any time through your browser settings or by responding to the consent prompt when it appears.

Security

Access is restricted by Forge scopes and least-privilege controls. Forge-issued tokens remain within Forge-managed context and are not persisted as credentials by SynapseWorks.

Your choices

You can uninstall a product at any time to revoke access tokens issued through Atlassian Forge. For data questions or deletion requests, contact us.
