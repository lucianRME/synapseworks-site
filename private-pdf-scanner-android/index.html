Private PDF scanning on Android

A private scanner should make clear where a document is processed, where it is kept, and what happens when you export it. RME: PDF & Document Scanner keeps its own scanning, editing, OCR and saved library workflow on the device.

From paper or file to a local document

Scan paper through Google ML Kit Document Scanner, or choose images and PDFs through Android's system picker. RME also accepts supported files sent through Android sharing. You can reorder, rotate, remove and filter pages before saving them. The source file you import remains with its original provider; RME makes a separate private copy only when you choose Save to RME.

That saved copy belongs to RME's on-device library. You can rename it, put it in a folder, search and sort saved documents, and reopen it later. Text from OCR you explicitly run can be indexed locally for search. An unsaved working session is temporary and is not a substitute for saving the document.

What “local” means when you share

RME does not operate a document backend or proprietary cloud sync service, and the Android app requires no RME account or advertising. A PDF or JPEG you export goes to the destination you choose with Android's file picker. If you pick a cloud-storage provider, that provider handles the exported file under its own terms. A file you share through Android's Sharesheet goes to the receiving app you select.

Practical boundary: local processing does not mean every external component is offline. Google Play services may need a connection to obtain or update scanner resources, and Google ML Kit may send technical diagnostics. RME's privacy policy explains these boundaries in detail.

When this approach is useful

Use a local library when you want to keep working with receipts, letters or forms on your Android device without an RME account. Run offline OCR when you need to copy recognized text or make a searchable PDF. Before sending sensitive files elsewhere, check the destination provider's storage and sharing settings; RME cannot control those after export.
