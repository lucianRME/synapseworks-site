Chrome Extensions

Small, practical browser tools for safer everyday workflows.

Submit Guard

A lightweight Chrome extension that helps prevent accidental form submissions and irreversible workflow actions.

  • Local-only operation with no backend and no SynapseWorks account.
  • Settings stored locally in chrome.storage.local.
  • No form contents are stored or transmitted by the extension.

Android Apps

PageHarbor

An open-source Android document scanner with local OCR, searchable-PDF generation, and user-controlled export.

  • No account, ads, PageHarbor backend, or PageHarbor analytics.

Atlassian Apps

Early-stage tools exploring decision tracking, backlog refinement, and delivery visibility in Jira.

Decision Register for Jira

An early-stage Jira app experiment for surfacing decisions from issue history and supporting lightweight governance.

  • Forge-only: runs on Atlassian with no external backend or external storage.
  • Read-only patterns with limited preference storage.
  • No external network egress. Data stays within Atlassian.

Security-first product design

SynapseWorks documents trust boundaries per product instead of applying one platform claim to the whole portfolio.

  • Synapse for Jira: Forge-native runtime with no external backend, no external storage, and no shared secrets or PATs.
  • Decision Register for Jira: Forge-only with no external backend, no external storage, and no external network egress.
  • Submit Guard: Chrome extension with local-only processing, no backend, no account, and settings stored locally.
