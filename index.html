RME PDF Scanner is available on Google Play, and Submit Guard is available on the Chrome Web Store. Decision Register for Jira and Synapse for Jira are early-stage products, publicly available on Atlassian Marketplace. Compare the four products.

Where are the guides and FAQs?

The documentation hub brings together guides and product-specific help for all four products. For scanning and PDF questions, go straight to RME PDF Scanner Help & FAQ.

Each product has its own architecture and privacy information. Read the privacy disclosures and security and trust guide for the product you plan to use.
