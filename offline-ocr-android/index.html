RME: Private PDF Scanner · Android guide

Offline OCR on Android

OCR turns visible letters in an image into text a device can search or copy. In RME: Private PDF Scanner, Latin OCR is built in; Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Devanagari support use optional Google Play services models. Recognition runs on the Android device.

From scanned page to recognized text

Scan a paper page, import an image or PDF, or reopen a document saved in RME's local library. After you start OCR, RME presents the recognized text for review and correction. You can re-run OCR on one page or every page, and batch-recognize selected library documents. Choose Copy Text if you want the effective text on the Android clipboard.

Recognized or corrected text can be indexed in the private on-device library. Full-library search shows matching snippets and can jump directly to the matching page. Search quality depends on what OCR recognized or what you corrected; a visually readable scan can still contain recognition errors.

What makes a PDF searchable

A scan-only PDF is largely a set of page images. RME can generate a PDF with an invisible Unicode text layer based on its OCR result. Compatible PDF viewers can search or select that layer while the original page image remains visible. The layer is a transcription aid, not a guarantee of exact text, layout, or language coverage.

Check the result: low contrast, handwriting, unusual fonts and unsupported scripts may reduce recognition quality. Review important names, numbers and dates against the page image before using copied text or an exported searchable PDF.

Local processing and network boundaries

RME's OCR and searchable-PDF text layer are prepared on the device. The app has no RME document-processing backend and requests neither INTERNET nor ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE. Additional OCR models may be downloaded by Google Play services. Document recognition runs on-device. Google ML Kit's documented technical diagnostics may also be sent to Google, although its disclosure says document images and recognized text are not sent. See the full privacy policy for the precise distinction.
