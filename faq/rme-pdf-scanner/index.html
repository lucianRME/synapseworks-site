Getting started

Installation, accounts and your first document.

What can I do with RME PDF Scanner?

Scan paper or import PDFs and images, organize documents in a local library, recognize text and create searchable PDFs. RME also includes page editing, merge and split tools, Fit Upload Limit, Share-Safe copies, two-sided card copies and portable backup.

Where can I download RME?

Install from Google Play or download the APK from the latest stable GitHub release. Use the official links on the RME product page.

Link to this answer ↗
Which devices does RME support?

RME is an Android app and requires Android 8.0 or later. The camera scanning flow uses Google ML Kit Document Scanner, delivered through Google Play services. Scanner availability depends on those components and device compatibility; installing an APK alone does not supply Google Play services.

Link to this answer ↗
Do I need an account or subscription?

No RME account or sign-in is required. The current Android app has no subscription requirement and no advertising. Your local library is not tied to an RME cloud account.

Link to this answer ↗
How do I create and keep my first document?

Choose Scan for paper or Import for an existing PDF or image. Review the pages, then choose Save to RME to keep an editable local library copy. Use Save PDF or Share when you also need a file outside the app.

Link to this answer ↗
What is the difference between Save to RME and Save PDF?

Save to RME keeps a document in the app’s private library so you can reopen and organize it. Save PDF writes a separate file to a destination you choose in Android. Exporting a PDF is not a substitute for saving an editable library copy or creating a portable backup.

Link to this answer ↗
Can I switch between Google Play and the GitHub APK?

The builds use different signing certificates, so Android cannot install one channel over the other as an update. First create a portable RME backup and verify that you can access it. Removing an installation deletes its private library; restore your backup after installing the other channel.

Link to this answer ↗
Can Obtainium keep RME up to date?

Yes. In Obtainium, add https://github.com/lucianRME/rme-pdf-scanner and leave prereleases disabled to follow stable GitHub releases. Manual entry avoids Obtainium’s hosted redirect, whose page includes advertising and analytics scripts. Obtainium is a separate application with its own practices.

Yes. Add pages in the scanner and review them together in RME. You can also add pages to the active document before exporting a single PDF.

Is the scanner limited to 20 pages?

The interactive Google scanner flow is configured for up to 20 pages. This is not a universal limit on saved libraries, imported PDFs or portable backups. Larger imports have separate safety checks and depend on available storage and device resources.

How do I fix a crop or missing page edge?

Review and adjust the detected corners in the scanner before accepting the page. Include the whole sheet and leave some contrast between the paper and its background. If an accepted scan has cut off content, rescan that page with the full edge visible.

Why is my scan blurry?

Clean the lens, use even lighting, hold the phone steady and wait for focus before taking the scan. Move closer while keeping the whole page in view. Filters can improve contrast, but they cannot reliably recover characters that were out of focus in the original capture.

How can I reduce shadows and glare?

Use diffuse light from more than one direction, keep your hand and phone shadow away from the page and avoid reflections on glossy paper. Compare RME’s enhancement filters in the preview. If text remains obscured, recapturing the page is more reliable than applying a stronger filter.

How do I keep color or make a black-and-white scan?

In the page editor, compare Original, Auto enhance, Grayscale, Black and white and High contrast. Choose a treatment that keeps small text and important colored marks readable. You can review the result before exporting.

If scanning is unavailable, you can still try importing a supported PDF or image; report persistent problems with your Android and RME versions.

OCR & searchable text

Recognize, review, correct and search document text.

What is OCR, and when should I use it?

Optical character recognition turns text in page images into recognized text that you can review, copy or search. Run OCR when you need searchable library content, text extraction or a searchable PDF. Recognition can make mistakes, so check important names, amounts and identifiers.

Link to this answer ↗
How do I recognize text in a document?

Open a document and choose the text recognition action. Select the appropriate OCR language or script when prompted. Review the result and save the document if you want its text indexed in the local library.

Link to this answer ↗
Which OCR languages and scripts are supported?

Bundled Latin recognition covers English, Romanian, German, French, Italian, Spanish and other Latin-script languages. Optional models support Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Devanagari, including Hindi, Marathi and Nepali. Select a supported script appropriate to the document; not every writing system is supported.

Link to this answer ↗
What does Automatic mean in the OCR language settings?

Automatic uses your device language as a recommendation for a supported OCR script. It does not guarantee that every page’s language is detected correctly. You can select a different language for an individual operation and choose whether to make it your default.

Link to this answer ↗
Why does an OCR language need a download?

Latin recognition is bundled with RME. The optional Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Devanagari models are delivered through Google Play services and may need a download before use. Recognition runs on your device after the required model is available.

Link to this answer ↗
Does OCR work offline?

Latin recognition is bundled for on-device use. An optional script can be used on-device once its required model is available. Google Play services may still need a connection to acquire or update optional models or scanner components. See the privacy policy for the full boundary.

Link to this answer ↗
Can I correct a word that OCR recognized incorrectly?

Yes. Open Review text, edit the recognized text for the page and save the correction. The correction is local text data; it does not retouch the page image. Use the review controls to return to recognized text if you want to remove a correction.

Link to this answer ↗
Can I re-run OCR on one page or the whole document?

Yes. Review text offers re-recognition for the current page or the document. Existing manual corrections remain in use when raw recognition is refreshed. Review the result and explicitly revert a correction if you want to use the new recognized text instead.

Link to this answer ↗
Can I recognize text in several saved documents at once?

Yes. Select saved documents and choose Recognize text. You can recognize missing text or re-run OCR. The missing-text option skips pages with current recognized or corrected text; re-running refreshes raw recognition while retaining corrections. Install the chosen optional model before starting.

Link to this answer ↗
What happens if I cancel OCR or some pages fail?

RME reports completed, skipped and failed work. Cancelling a batch keeps completed page results. A failed refresh keeps existing text rather than treating the failed attempt as new recognition. Review unread or failed pages and retry those that need attention.

Link to this answer ↗
Can I copy or extract recognized text?

Yes. Use the text result and copy or text export actions offered by RME. Check the output before using it elsewhere. Once copied or shared, the receiving app or clipboard handles that text outside RME’s private library.

Link to this answer ↗
What is a searchable PDF?

A searchable PDF combines visible page content with a recognized-text layer so a compatible PDF viewer can find and select text. Choose the searchable PDF export action. A regular PDF made from images is not automatically searchable merely because it opens in a PDF viewer.

Link to this answer ↗
Why does OCR miss words, handwriting or table structure?

Recognition depends on image sharpness, text size, orientation, contrast and the selected script. Handwriting and complex layouts may be unreliable. Rescan unclear pages, rotate them correctly and review the text. OCR is not a guarantee of exact transcription or editable Word or Excel formatting.

Documents saved with Save to RME live in the app’s private local storage, together with library metadata, thumbnails and any OCR text you saved. They are not automatically ordinary PDF files in Downloads. Export a PDF or export the library when you need files outside RME.

Use the document’s library menu to rename it or move it to another library folder. A library rename or move does not update PDFs that you already exported to an outside destination.

Yes. Use the library’s sort control to choose an available order, such as title or document timestamps. Sorting changes how documents are listed, not the page order inside a document.

Yes. Library search looks at document titles and saved recognized text across the library. OCR content must first be recognized and saved. Results can identify the matching page so you can open the relevant part of a document.

The page may not have saved OCR text, recognition may have failed or the recognized spelling may differ from the image. Open the document, run or review OCR, save changes and search again. RME does not silently run OCR on every imported file just because it is in the library.

RME can suggest document names locally using available document context and recognized text. Review the suggestion before applying it. Smart naming does not require an RME cloud account or send document text to a naming server.

Fit Upload Limit

Prepare readable PDF copies for size-limited websites and forms.

How do I make a PDF fit an upload limit?

Open Fit upload limit, choose the document and set the maximum file size. Prepare the PDF, then inspect the final size and page preview before saving or sharing the copy.

Link to this answer ↗
Which file-size targets can I choose?

Presets include 200 KB, 500 KB, 1 MB and 2 MB. You can also enter a custom limit. Check the destination website’s accepted file type, number of files and maximum size for each individual upload.

Link to this answer ↗
Why can RME not reach the size I requested?

Page count, detail and readable image quality impose practical limits. RME will not keep reducing quality below its minimum settings just to claim success. Choose a larger limit, remove unnecessary pages or split the submission if the receiving website permits it.

Link to this answer ↗
Will a smaller PDF lose detail?

It can. RME uses readability-aware processing and warns when fine detail may be reduced. Zoom into small print, numbers, stamps and photographs in the prepared preview. Meeting a byte limit does not guarantee that a person or upload portal will accept the document.

Link to this answer ↗
What if the PDF is already below the limit?

RME avoids unnecessary recompression when a document already fits. Check the final result and size rather than repeatedly reducing a file that already meets the destination’s requirement.

Link to this answer ↗
Does fitting a limit overwrite my original?

The workflow prepares an output copy. Your source document remains separate. Save the prepared file to the destination you choose, using a filename that makes the intended submission copy easy to identify.

Link to this answer ↗
Why is a PDF too complex or protected for Fit Upload Limit?

Some PDFs contain protection or features that RME cannot safely preserve in a size-limited copy. The tool may refuse them rather than silently dropping required content. Use an authorized simpler export from the source application or another appropriate PDF tool.

Share-Safe & redaction

What does Share-Safe do?

Share-Safe prepares a separate PDF copy with optional destructive blackout redaction, a purpose/date watermark and a file-size limit. Choose the workflow that fits your task, such as a portal upload, an ID copy, email or archive, and inspect the final preview.

How do I black out sensitive information?

Open Share-Safe, add blackout regions over the details you want to remove and review each affected page. Adjust the regions carefully, then prepare the copy. The redactions are flattened destructively into the prepared output; your saved source document remains separate.

Can someone remove a Share-Safe blackout to reveal the hidden area?

The exported redacted copy is prepared with the selected areas destructively removed, rather than covered by a removable annotation. Still review the final file and every page: information outside the selected areas, repeated elsewhere or visible in unredacted context can remain in the copy.

How do I add a purpose and date watermark?

Can I choose where the watermark appears?

Link to this answer ↗
Can I redact, watermark and fit an upload limit in one workflow?

Yes. Share-Safe can combine those options in a prepared copy. Size fitting uses the sanitized and watermarked result. Review both the remaining information and readability; if the target is too small, choose another limit.

Save or share that prepared copy rather than the original.

How do I back up my RME library?

Open Backup & restore, create a portable backup and choose its destination in Android’s picker. Wait for completion and keep the finished file somewhere you can access independently of the app, preferably away from the phone you are protecting.

Link to this answer ↗
What does a portable backup preserve?

A portable backup preserves the library’s nested folders, documents, editable page assets, page order and edits, saved OCR state and retained imported source assets. It is designed for rebuilding an RME library rather than simply providing a folder of readable PDFs.

Link to this answer ↗
Does a backup include app lock and all app settings?

No. The portable format does not include app-lock credentials or settings, general preferences, caches or an unsaved active session. Thumbnails and search indexes are rebuilt during restore. Configure security and preferences on the destination device as needed.

Link to this answer ↗
Should I use Export library or a portable backup?

Use Export library for ordinary PDFs and folders that you can open outside RME. Use a portable backup to restore an editable RME library with its metadata and saved OCR state. You may want both for different purposes.

Can I protect a backup with a password?

Yes. Enable password protection when creating the backup. The encrypted backup protects the complete archive, including document titles, folder names and OCR text. Keep the password separately and securely; do not assume that app lock also encrypts exported backups.

Can RME recover a forgotten backup password?

RME does not store the backup password and has no account-based password recovery. Keep it securely before relying on the encrypted file. If the password is lost, you need another accessible backup or the original documents.

How do I restore a backup?

Link to this answer ↗
How do I move to a new phone?

On the old phone, create a verified portable backup and transfer the completed file using a method you trust. Install RME on the new Android phone and restore that file. Check documents and folders on the new phone before removing anything from the old one.

Does RME automatically back up to Google or an RME server?

A failed or cancelled restore before activation leaves the existing library unchanged. Keep the original backup and check the password, file integrity and free space. Uninstalling RME or clearing its data removes the private library, so create an accessible backup before either action.

Privacy & app lock

Understand local processing, permissions and outside services.

Does RME upload my documents or recognized text?

RME has no backend that receives scanned documents, PDFs, OCR text or library metadata. Document-content processing runs on the device. A file you explicitly share or save to an outside provider is handled by that provider under its own policies.

Link to this answer ↗
Why does RME not request Internet or broad storage permissions?

The current app uses local processing, private app storage, Android’s system picker and temporary file-sharing grants. RME itself declares neither INTERNET nor ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and does not request broad storage access. Google Play services and receiving apps have their own capabilities and permissions.

Link to this answer ↗
Can Google components still use the network?

Yes. Google Play services may acquire or update scanner resources and optional OCR models. ML Kit may send encrypted technical diagnostics under Google’s practices; its documented diagnostics do not include document images or recognized text. This is separate from RME-operated analytics, which RME does not run.

Link to this answer ↗
Is saving to a cloud provider still private?

You choose whether to give the exported file to a provider offered by Android. That provider then handles storage, synchronization, accounts and retention under its own terms. Choosing a cloud destination is different from RME uploading the private library to its own server.

Link to this answer ↗
How do I protect RME with a PIN or biometrics?

Open App lock in RME’s security settings. RME uses Android device authentication, such as the device PIN, pattern, password or supported strong biometrics. Set up a device lock in Android first if needed; RME does not create a separate app password.

Link to this answer ↗
Does app lock encrypt the local library?

No. App lock protects access through RME’s interface; it does not encrypt the local library at rest. Android manages device credentials and biometric templates. Use password protection separately when you need an encrypted portable backup.

Link to this answer ↗
When does RME lock again?

The app-lock settings let you choose locking immediately after leaving RME, after one minute or after five minutes. You can also use Lock now. Choose the setting that fits how you share or use your device.

How do I remove my documents and app data?

Delete saved documents in the library, or use Android’s clear-app-data or uninstall action to remove RME’s private data. These actions do not delete copies you exported, shared or backed up elsewhere. Delete those separately at their chosen destinations. Read the full privacy policy for retention details.

Updates & support

Find help and report a reproducible problem safely.

How do I update RME safely?

Use the same installation channel you already use: Google Play, or the latest stable GitHub APK through your chosen update method. Keep a verified portable backup before major changes. Switching between Play and GitHub requires extra care because their signing certificates differ.

Why can I not find a feature described here?

Check that you are using a recent stable RME release and the relevant document screen. Some actions need saved documents, a selected page or an installed OCR model. If the feature still appears missing, contact support with your app version and a description of the screen.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email support@synapseworks.org or open an issue in the RME issue tracker. Include the RME version, Android version, device model, the steps you followed, what you expected and what happened. Use synthetic or fully anonymized examples.

Should I attach identity cards or private documents to a support request?

No. Do not post sensitive scans, OCR text, document names or personal information in public issues. Reproduce the problem with synthetic or anonymized material where possible. Report privacy or security concerns privately to security@synapseworks.org.

Why do I still see the PageHarbor name in a link or package ID?

RME was formerly called PageHarbor. The Android application ID remains org.synapseworks.pageharbor for upgrade compatibility. Use the official RME product and download links when checking the identity of the app.
